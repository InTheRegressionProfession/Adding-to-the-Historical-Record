 A political cartoon by J. L. Dufresne from 1903. The central figure is a winged woman representing France, dressed in a white tunic with blue and red accents, a golden breastplate, and a red Phrygian cap. She holds a sword in her left hand and points her right hand towards a man in a dark suit, Maurice Rouvier, who is leaning on a wooden podium. In the background, a chaotic battle scene unfolds with soldiers in Prussian-style helmets and a city skyline under smoke. To the left, a woman in a dark dress and a man in a blue military uniform with a red collar stand watching. The bottom of the cartoon shows a decorative frieze with a woman and a figure holding a sword.

*Séance du 23 Novembre 1903.*

**LE DÉSARMEMENT DE LA FRANCE**
